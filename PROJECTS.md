# Selected Projects

## Cyclic Peptide–JAK Selectivity Screening
**Graduate computational research | JAK1, JAK2, JAK3 | ~160,000 CX4C peptides**

Workflow: large-scale docking, cross-target ranking, selectivity analysis, amino-acid analysis, classification/regression modeling, candidate prioritization, structural interpretation, and exploratory short MD.

## Acetylcholinesterase
Human AChE structural analysis, donepezil reference docking, and pyridoquinoxaline candidate evaluation.

## Human Pancreatic Lipase
Docking and binding-site analysis with interpretation of Ser152, Asp176, and His263.

## α-Amylase / α-Glucosidase
Structure-based docking of vitexin and isovitexin with catalytic-site interpretation.

## Leishmania CYP51
Azole docking and structural analysis of interactions with the CYP51 heme environment.
